General

What does Avyentra Technologies do?

We are a blockchain and software development company. We work across artificial intelligence and automation, software engineering, blockchain and Web3, cloud and digital infrastructure, cybersecurity and digital transformation.

Where is Avyentra located?

Our locations are Mohali in India, Dubai in the United Arab Emirates, and Adelaide in Australia. We work with clients around the world.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with startups, growing businesses and established companies across many industries.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. You can browse selected projects on our Work page.

Services

Do you build blockchain and Web3 products?

Yes. Blockchain is a core part of our work, including smart contracts, dApps and Web3 platforms. See our blockchain development services.

Do you build apps for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We build native and cross-platform mobile apps, and we can help you choose the right approach. Read our guide to native and cross-platform apps.

Can you add AI to my existing software?

Often, yes. We review your software and data first, then suggest where AI can help. Learn more about our AI development services.

Can you redesign or improve an existing product?

Yes. We can review your current website or app and recommend what to improve first.

Do you provide blockchain and cloud services?

Yes. Our services include blockchain development and cloud solutions.

Process

How do I start a project with Avyentra?

Share your idea through our contact page, by email or on WhatsApp. We offer a free consultation and reply within 24 hours.

What information should I prepare?

It helps to share your goal, target users, main features, budget range and preferred timeline. A rough idea is fine, and we can help you shape it.

Will I be able to review the work during the project?

Yes. You review the plan before development, and we build and test in small, visible steps.

Who will I work with?

You will work with our blockchain and software engineers as one team, from planning through launch.

Pricing and Timelines

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on scope, features, platforms and design depth. After we understand your project, we provide a clear estimate. This guide explains what affects app cost.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the size of the project. We share a proposed timeline with your estimate, once the scope is clear.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation is free, and you can use it to discuss your idea and next steps.

Support

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We can help with fixes, updates and new features after your product goes live.

Will you publish my app to the stores?

Yes. We can handle store listings, review and submission for the App Store and Google Play.

Can you maintain a product built by another team?

Often, yes. We first review the code and setup, then tell you what we can support.

Security and Data

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share confidential details.

How do you handle security during development?

Access control, testing and safe handling of data are part of our everyday work. See our cybersecurity services.

Is my business data safe when you build AI tools?

We review where your data lives, who can access it, and how each provider uses it before any AI tool is connected.
