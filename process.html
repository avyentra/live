Questions

Common questions about our process

More answers on our FAQs page.

How long does a typical project take?

A simple website or app takes 4 to 8 weeks. A full platform with blockchain or AI usually takes 3 to 6 months. You get a clear timeline in the proposal.

Will I see progress during the project?

Yes. You get a weekly update and a live demo every two weeks, and you can follow every task on a shared board.

What if I want to change something mid-way?

Changes are normal. We plan them into the next sprint and tell you upfront if they affect time or cost.

Who owns the code and design?

You do. After final payment, all code, designs and accounts are handed over to you.
